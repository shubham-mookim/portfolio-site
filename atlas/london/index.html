London

Loud, expensive, and at night genuinely one of the best-lit places I have stood in.

Piccadilly Circus · 51.51° N — after dark

The lit crossroads

Piccadilly at night is a light source before it is a place. Everyone stands in roughly the same spot for roughly the same photograph and I am not above it — I went and stood there too, and it was worth it.

Free museums

The National Gallery costs nothing. You can walk in off Trafalgar Square, stand under that dome, look at paintings for an hour and leave. I think about this more than I expected to.

Old stone

The Tower is nine hundred years old and sits in the middle of a financial district, which is a very London way to treat a castle.
