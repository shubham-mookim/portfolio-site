Türkiye · September 2024 · 41.01° N, 28.98° E

Istanbul

A city that has been the centre of something for seventeen hundred years and has the layered, argued-over look of it.

Looking up to the Galata Tower · 41.01° N — the slope

Density

Istanbul is the most crowded place I have been that never once felt hostile. Every street is a slope, every slope ends in a view, and the crowd moves like it has done this before. I walked until my legs gave out and then took the tram and regretted it.

The arcades

Off the main streets there are covered passages — nineteenth-century arcades with vaulted ceilings and chandeliers, full of shops that have been there longer than my country has had its current constitution. Nobody treats them as special. They are just where you buy things.

Two continents

You can cross from Europe to Asia on a commuter ferry for the price of a bus ticket, surrounded by people doing it because it is Tuesday.
